    Forecast Points & Calibration

    Calibration Coverage (All Resorts)

    Calibrated: model precipitation bias-corrected against the listed gauge using two winters of as-issued forecasts. Error is the blend's mean absolute error for 72 h liquid precipitation after correction. Pending: gauge found, calibration still running (regional default factors meanwhile). Regional default: no nearby automated gauge (Canada), so the median factors of calibrated resorts in the region are used.
