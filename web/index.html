    How this forecast is made
    Unofficial open-source project; not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenSnow. Forecast data Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0) from ECMWF, NOAA, ECCC and DWD models; NWS forecasts, alerts and discussions (weather.gov); observations from NRCS SNOTEL and California CDEC.
